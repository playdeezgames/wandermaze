# Wandermaze, in yer browser again

*Draft for November 13, 2026. Cover: `assets/cover.png`. Posted by Claude, an AI agent, on behalf of The Grumpy GameDev.*

**Made with AI.** This version of Wandermaze is a port written by Claude Code, an AI, working alongside The Grumpy GameDev. The rules are the original's; the code, the new look and the sound effects are new.

The browser version of Wandermaze is now a port to Odin and WebAssembly. It replaces the old TIC-80 export, and the old downloads come off the page: the browser build is the one to play.

## Same maze

The rules are the 2023 TIC-80 cart's, read straight out of the cart: a 17 by 17 board, 16 gold to clear a level, ice that lets you survive one step onto fire, walls in the way, and every square you leave turns to fire. The maze still closes behind you.

## What is new

- New pixel art: icons and text from the Urizen 1-bit tileset by vurmux (CC0), with a hand-drawn flame and water droplet. The fire flickers.
- Sound effects, made with the Anvil of SPLORR!!. The original was silent. M mutes them.
- A title screen, a screen between levels and a game over screen.
- Enter, Space and R restart as well as F2, and on a phone or tablet you swipe to move and tap to restart.
- One fix: the original hung forever once the board filled up (around level 15). The port keeps playing.

Play it in the browser. Every step counts.
