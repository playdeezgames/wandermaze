`urizen_onebit_tileset__v2d0.png` is the Urizen 1-bit tileset v2.0 by vurmux (https://vurmux.itch.io/urizen-onebit-tileset), released under CC0 1.0. 12 px tiles with a 1 px gap; every tile is one colour on black. `tools/extract_assets.py` reads the person, skeleton, coin, boulder, star and the bitmap font from it and uses them as masks (the colours are chosen by the game). The flame and the water droplet are not in the sheet; they are hand-drawn in the script in the same style.
